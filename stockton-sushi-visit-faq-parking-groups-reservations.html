Visiting Raw Sushi Bar in Stockton: Your Questions Answered

Planning a visit to Raw Sushi Bar on Trinity Parkway in Stockton? Here are straight answers on reservations, groups, corkage, and more.

If you're planning a visit to Raw Sushi Bar at 10742 Trinity Parkway Suite D, Stockton, CA 95219, a few practical questions come up more than others. Here are direct answers, based on our actual house policies.

Do you take reservations?

Yes — call us at (209) 954-9729 to reserve a table. We don't currently run online reservations, so a phone call is the fastest way to confirm a time, especially for weekend evenings or larger groups.

Can our group split the check?

We're happy to split checks for parties of four or fewer. For larger groups, we ask that the table settle the bill together, so it helps to sort out who's covering what before the meal wraps up.

Is there a corkage fee if we bring wine?

Yes, corkage is $18 per bottle. Outside food and beverages otherwise aren't permitted, so if you're bringing a bottle for a special occasion, plan on the corkage fee rather than trying to bring in outside snacks or drinks.

What if we're celebrating a birthday with a cake?

You're welcome to bring a cake for the table — there's a $12 cake fee to have it served. It's an easy way to work a celebration into a regular dinner reservation.

Where do we park?

Raw Sushi Bar is in the Trinity Parkway retail corridor in Stockton, in a shared lot in front of the suite. It's a straightforward walk-up from parking to the door, which makes it easy for families and larger groups traveling in more than one car.

One more thing to know

Like any restaurant serving raw fish, we're required to note: the consumption of raw or undercooked eggs, meat, poultry, seafood, or shellfish may increase your risk of foodborne illness. If you'd rather stick to cooked dishes, our cooked-menu guide covers what's available.
