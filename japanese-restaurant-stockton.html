Authentic Japanese Dining in Stockton

Raw Sushi Bar is more than a sushi restaurant. We are Stockton's home for authentic Japanese cuisine, offering a menu that spans the full breadth of traditional and modern Japanese cooking. Founded in 2005 by Hoang, who grew up immersed in Japanese culinary traditions, our restaurant was built on a simple principle: serve real Japanese food made with the freshest ingredients and genuine care.

Our kitchen prepares everything from delicate nigiri and sashimi platters to hearty teriyaki chicken and salmon entrees, crispy shrimp and vegetable tempura, rich tonkotsu-style ramen bowls, and savory gyoza. Each dish reflects the balance of flavor, texture, and presentation that defines Japanese cooking. Whether you are craving a light lunch of edamame and miso soup on Friday or a full dinner spread on Saturday evening, our menu has something for every appetite.

At the bar, our bartenders craft cocktails with Japanese whisky, premium sake, and fresh ingredients that pair beautifully with sushi and grilled dishes alike. If you are searching for a top-rated sushi experience or a complete Japanese dinner in Stockton, Raw Sushi Bar delivers on both fronts. Explore our full menu to see everything we offer.

A Menu Built for Everyone

One of the things that makes Raw Sushi Bar a standout Japanese restaurant in Stockton is the diversity of our menu. Sushi purists will find an extensive selection of nigiri, sashimi, and maki rolls made with fish sourced daily. But our menu goes well beyond the sushi bar. We serve grilled teriyaki plates with chicken, beef, salmon, and tofu options. Our tempura is lightly battered and fried to a perfect golden crisp. And for noodle lovers, our udon and ramen bowls are satisfying and deeply flavorful.

We also cater to guests with dietary preferences. Vegetarian rolls, gluten-conscious options, and lighter dishes like seaweed salad and sunomono make it easy for everyone at the table to find something they love. Families, couples on a date night, and groups of friends all feel at home here.
