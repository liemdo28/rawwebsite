Downtown Modesto Dining Guide

By Raw Sushi Bar Team | April 10, 2026 | 6 min read

Downtown Modesto has quietly become one of the Central Valley's most interesting places to eat. Anchored by the energy of I Street and surrounded by a mix of historic architecture and modern storefronts, the neighborhood offers a dining scene that rewards exploration. Whether you work downtown and need a solid lunch spot, or you are looking for a destination dinner, there is plenty to discover.

As one of downtown Modesto's most established restaurants, Raw Sushi Bar has had a front-row seat to the area's growth. Here is our guide to getting the most out of dining in this part of the city.

Sushi in Downtown Modesto

Raw Sushi Bar sits at 1200 I Street, right in the heart of Modesto's downtown corridor. With over 791 reviews and a loyal following that spans the entire Central Valley, it has become the destination for anyone craving fresh, expertly prepared Japanese cuisine in the area.

The menu covers the full range of Japanese dining. Traditional nigiri and sashimi are prepared with fish sourced fresh daily. Specialty rolls offer creative combinations for adventurous eaters. And a selection of cooked entrees, including teriyaki, tempura, and udon, rounds out the options for guests who prefer something warm and familiar.

What keeps people coming back is the consistency. Whether you visit on a Tuesday afternoon or a packed Saturday evening, the quality never wavers. Every piece of fish is cut with precision, every roll is assembled with care, and every plate leaves the kitchen looking as good as it tastes.

"Fresh fish, prepared with care, served with pride. That is the standard we set for ourselves every single day at our Modesto location. Our guests deserve nothing less."

The I Street Experience

I Street is the backbone of downtown Modesto's dining and entertainment district. The walkable stretch is lined with locally owned restaurants, cafes, and shops that give the area a character you will not find in a strip mall. On any given evening, you can stroll past art galleries, grab a coffee, and settle in for dinner all within a few blocks.

Raw Sushi Bar fits naturally into this landscape. The restaurant draws a mix of downtown professionals, families from across Modesto, and visitors who have heard about the food and want to experience it firsthand. The I Street location makes it easy to combine dinner with a walk through the neighborhood, catching live music at a nearby venue, or browsing the shops before or after your meal.

Parking is readily available in the surrounding lots and along nearby streets, so getting to the restaurant is straightforward even during the busiest evenings.

Lunch in Downtown Modesto

For the thousands of people who work in and around downtown Modesto, finding a reliable lunch spot that is quick enough for a workday but good enough to actually enjoy can be a challenge. Raw Sushi Bar solves that problem. The restaurant is open for lunch Tuesday through Friday, making it a perfect midday escape for professionals in the area.

Lunch at Raw moves at a pace that respects your schedule. The kitchen is fast without being rushed, and the menu includes options that work whether you have 30 minutes or a full hour. Bento boxes offer a satisfying combination of sushi, salad, and a cooked item. Individual rolls and nigiri plates let you customize a lighter meal. And for those days when you want something more substantial, the teriyaki and tempura entrees deliver.

Lunch Hours

Tuesday through Friday. Perfect for a midday break from the office or a catch-up with colleagues.

Quick and Quality

The kitchen is efficient without cutting corners. Your meal arrives fresh, plated beautifully, and ready to enjoy.

Dinner and Special Occasions

When the sun goes down, downtown Modesto takes on a different energy, and Raw Sushi Bar transforms right along with it. The evening atmosphere is warm and inviting, with lighting that sets the mood for a relaxed dinner whether you are celebrating an anniversary, hosting a birthday, or simply enjoying a night out with friends.

The dinner menu expands to include the full range of signature rolls, sashimi platters, and premium entrees. Cocktails and sake selections complement the food beautifully, and the staff is attentive without being intrusive. It is the kind of dining experience where you can settle in for the evening and let each course unfold at its own pace.

For special occasions, consider ordering a sashimi platter to share as a centerpiece. The presentation alone is worth celebrating, and the freshness of the fish speaks for itself. Pair it with a few specialty rolls and a round of cocktails, and you have an evening that everyone at the table will remember.

"There is something special about dinner in downtown Modesto. The neighborhood has a warmth to it, and we try to reflect that same feeling every time a guest sits down at our table."
