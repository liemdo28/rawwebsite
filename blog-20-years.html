🎉 ANNIVERSARY SPECIAL

20 Years of Excellence: Our Journey

Published February 15, 2025 | By Chef Takeshi Yamamoto | 8 min read

As I stand behind the sushi bar today, watching my team carefully craft each roll with the same dedication we had on our opening day, I'm overwhelmed with gratitude. Twenty years ago, we opened the doors of Raw Sushi Bistro with a simple dream: to bring authentic Japanese sushi craftsmanship to California while honoring the traditions passed down through generations.

The Beginning: A Dream Takes Shape

In 2005, Stockton was hungry for authentic Japanese cuisine. The city had sushi restaurants, but we envisioned something different—a place where traditional edomae-style sushi met California's innovative spirit, where every ingredient was sourced with integrity, and where guests would feel like they were dining at a hidden gem in Tokyo.

I remember our first day vividly. We had eight seats at the sushi bar, four tables, and a team of three. My hands were trembling as I prepared the first order—a simple nigiri set. That guest, Mrs. Chen, is still a regular today. She tells everyone who'll listen that she "discovered" us on day one.

"Success isn't measured in years, but in the relationships you build and the traditions you honor. Every piece of sushi is a conversation between chef and guest."

The Milestones That Shaped Us

2005

The Grand Opening

We opened with a simple menu of 30 items, focusing on perfecting the fundamentals. Our philosophy: master the basics before attempting innovation.

2007

Building Our Reputation

Word spread quickly. Lines formed outside on weekends. We were featured in the San Francisco Chronicle's "Hidden Gems" series, putting us on the map for sushi enthusiasts across Northern California.

2010

Recognition & Expansion

Named "Best Sushi Restaurant in California" by Bay Area Food Critics Association. We expanded our space, doubling our seating capacity and introducing our first omakase menu.

2012

Sustainable Seafood Initiative

We partnered with sustainable fisheries, becoming one of the first sushi restaurants in California to commit to ocean-friendly sourcing. This wasn't just good business—it was the right thing to do.

2015

A Million Thank Yous

We celebrated serving our one-millionth guest. What started as eight seats had grown into a beloved community institution. We introduced our sake pairing program with over 50 premium selections.

2018

Training the Next Generation

Launched our apprenticeship program, training young chefs in traditional sushi arts. Many of our apprentices now run their own successful restaurants across California.

2020

Adapting & Thriving

Like everyone, we faced unprecedented challenges. But our community rallied around us. We pivoted to offering premium takeout and DIY sushi kits, staying connected with our guests in new ways.

2023

Innovation Meets Tradition

Introduced our seasonal tasting menu, changing monthly to showcase the best ingredients each season offers. Our wine and sake program earned recognition from Wine Spectator.

2025

Twenty Years Strong

Here we are. Twenty years of early mornings at the fish market, countless hours perfecting rice, and immeasurable joy in serving our community. We're launching our anniversary menu featuring dishes that tell our story.

The Philosophy That Guides Us

Over two decades, our core philosophy hasn't wavered:

Respect for Ingredients

Every fish, every grain of rice, every sheet of nori deserves respect. We source the finest ingredients and let their natural flavors shine. Our relationships with suppliers span decades—they know our standards and share our commitment to quality.

Honor Tradition, Embrace Innovation

We study classical techniques daily, yet we're not afraid to innovate. Our "California Dreaming" roll—a signature creation—honors both Japanese tradition and California's culinary creativity with sustainable bluefin, avocado, and microgreens from local farms.

Community First

We're not just a restaurant; we're part of Stockton's fabric. From supporting local schools to hosting community events, we believe in giving back to the place that embraced us.

Lessons from 20 Years

If I could go back to 2005 and give my younger self advice, I'd say:

Stay humble. No matter how many awards we win, there's always something new to learn. I still discover new techniques, new flavor combinations, new ways to improve.

Invest in people. Our team isn't just staff—they're family. Some have been with us for 15+ years. Their dedication, creativity, and passion are the real secret to our success.

Quality never compromises. There were times when cutting costs was tempting. We never did. Using the best ingredients, maintaining the highest standards—these aren't negotiable.

Listen to your guests. The feedback, both praise and criticism, has shaped who we are. Our most popular dishes often came from guest suggestions or conversations at the sushi bar.

Looking Forward

As we celebrate this milestone, we're excited about the future. We're planning:

  • An Anniversary Tasting Menu: Twelve courses celebrating our journey, featuring signature dishes from each era of our history.
  • Master Classes: Sharing our knowledge with the community through sushi-making workshops.
  • A Cookbook: Documenting our recipes, techniques, and stories for home cooks and future generations.
  • Expanded Sustainability Efforts: Partnering with ocean conservation organizations and continuing to lead in sustainable seafood practices.

Thank You

To our guests: Thank you for trusting us with your celebrations, your date nights, your family dinners. For bringing your children who are now bringing their children. For traveling across California to dine with us. For your patience during the busiest nights and your encouragement during the toughest times.

To our team: You are the heart of Raw Sushi Bistro. Your skill, dedication, and passion make the magic happen every single day.

To Stockton: Thank you for welcoming a young chef with a dream and supporting us for twenty incredible years.

Here's to the next twenty years of crafting perfect nigiri, rolling memorable experiences, and serving our community with excellence.

Join us this year as we celebrate with special events, exclusive menu items, and surprises throughout 2025. Because this milestone isn't just ours—it's yours, too.

Itadakimasu. Let's eat together.

— Chef Takeshi Yamamoto
Founder & Head Chef, Raw Sushi Bistro
