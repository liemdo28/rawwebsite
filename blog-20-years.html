🎉 ANNIVERSARY SPECIAL

20 Years of Excellence: Our Journey

Published February 15, 2025 | By Hoang, Founder | 8 min read

As I stand in the restaurant today, watching our team carefully craft each roll with the same dedication we had on our opening day, I'm overwhelmed with gratitude. Twenty years ago, we opened the doors of Raw Sushi Bistro with a simple dream: to carry on a family tradition of Japanese fine cuisine and bring exceptional sushi to our community.

The Beginning: A Dream Takes Shape

In 2005, I left my previous career behind to follow my true passion. I knew that Stockton was hungry for authentic, high-quality Japanese cuisine—a place where traditional techniques met California's innovative spirit, and where every ingredient was sourced with absolute integrity.

I remember our first day vividly. We started small, focusing entirely on perfecting the fundamentals. We had a tight-knit team, but what we lacked in size, we made up for in passion and an uncompromising standard for quality.

"Success isn't measured in years, but in the relationships you build and the traditions you honor. Every piece of sushi is a conversation between chef and guest."

The Milestones That Shaped Us

2005

The Grand Opening

We opened our doors in Stockton with a simple menu, focusing on perfecting the fundamentals. Our philosophy: master the basics before attempting innovation.

2010

Building Our Reputation

Word spread quickly through the community. Thanks to our loyal guests, we became known as the go-to destination in Stockton for fresh, high-quality sushi and our signature warm hospitality.

2015

Expanding the Menu

We introduced many of the signature rolls that our guests love today, like the Dante's Inferno and the Reggie's Roll, perfectly blending traditional cuts with bold, modern flavor profiles.

2018

Growing to Modesto

Driven by demand from guests traveling from out of town, we proudly opened our second location in downtown Modesto, bringing the Raw Sushi Bistro experience to a whole new community.

2020

Adapting & Thriving

Like everyone, we faced unprecedented challenges. But our community rallied around us. We pivoted to offering premium takeout and delivery, staying connected with our guests through difficult times.

2025

Twenty Years Strong

Here we are. Twenty years of sourcing the best fish, perfecting our craft, and finding immeasurable joy in serving our community across two locations.

The Philosophy That Guides Us

Over two decades, our core philosophy hasn't wavered:

Respect for Ingredients

Every fish, every grain of rice, every sheet of nori deserves respect. We source the finest ingredients and let their natural flavors shine. Our relationships with our suppliers span decades—they know our standards and share our commitment to quality.

Honor Tradition, Embrace Innovation

We respect classical techniques, yet we're not afraid to innovate. Our signature rolls—like Dante's Inferno and the Roadkill Roll—honor both Japanese tradition and California's culinary creativity.

Community First

We're not just a restaurant; we're part of the fabric of Stockton and Modesto. We believe in giving back to the places that have embraced us for the last twenty years.

Lessons from 20 Years

If I could go back to 2005 and give my younger self advice, I'd say:

Stay humble. No matter how long we've been doing this, there's always something new to learn. We continuously look for new ways to improve the guest experience.

Invest in people. Our team isn't just staff—they're family. Their dedication, creativity, and passion are the real secret to our continued success.

Quality never compromises. There were times when cutting costs was tempting. We never did. Using the best ingredients and maintaining the highest standards—these aren't negotiable.

Listen to your guests. The feedback, both praise and criticism, has shaped who we are. Our most popular dishes often came from guest suggestions.

Looking Forward

As we celebrate this milestone, we're excited about the future. We will continue to innovate, source sustainably, and provide the absolute best dining experience for every person who walks through our doors.

Thank You

To our guests: Thank you for trusting us with your celebrations, your date nights, your family dinners. For bringing your children who are now bringing their children. For traveling across California to dine with us. For your patience during the busiest nights and your encouragement during the toughest times.

To our team: You are the heart of Raw Sushi Bistro. Your skill, dedication, and passion make the magic happen every single day.

To Stockton and Modesto: Thank you for welcoming us with a dream and supporting us for twenty incredible years.

Here's to the next twenty years of crafting perfect nigiri, rolling memorable experiences, and serving our community with excellence.

Join us this year as we celebrate our 20th anniversary. Because this milestone isn't just ours—it's yours, too.

Itadakimasu. Let's eat together.

— Hoang
Founder, Raw Sushi Bistro
