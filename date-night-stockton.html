Why Raw Sushi Bar Is Stockton's Best Date Night Spot

Finding the right restaurant for a date night in Stockton can be a challenge. You want somewhere with great food, a relaxed yet sophisticated atmosphere, and enough variety to make the evening feel special. Raw Sushi Bar checks every box. Our warm, modern interior creates the perfect setting for conversation, and our menu offers a shared dining experience that feels both adventurous and comfortable.

Sushi is inherently social. Sharing a platter of signature rolls, passing pieces of nigiri back and forth, and exploring new flavors together makes for a dining experience that connects people. At Raw Sushi Bar, couples can start with edamame and miso soup, move on to a sashimi plate or a selection of specialty rolls like the BTS Roll or Rainbow Roll, and finish with a perfectly crafted cocktail. It is a meal built for two.

With 4.5 stars on Yelp and over 655 reviews, Raw Sushi Bar has earned its reputation as one of the best restaurants in Stockton. Couples return week after week because the food is consistently excellent and the service makes every visit feel personal. Whether it is your first date or your hundredth, this is the place to be.

Craft Cocktails & Dishes for Two

No date night is complete without great drinks, and our bar program delivers. Our bartenders craft cocktails using premium Japanese whisky, top-shelf sake, and fresh ingredients. Pair a sake-based cocktail with a plate of salmon sashimi, or enjoy a Japanese whisky highball alongside our Dante's Inferno Roll. For wine lovers, we offer a curated selection that complements both sushi and grilled entrees.

For the ideal date night dinner, we suggest starting with our Chef's Choice Sashimi Platter to share, followed by two or three specialty rolls from our full menu. Add a teriyaki or tempura plate if you want something heartier. Our staff is always happy to recommend pairings and help you build the perfect meal for the evening.

We also offer Happy Hour specials Monday through Thursday from 4:30 to 6:00 PM and Friday through Sunday from 2:30 to 5:30 PM, making an early date night both delicious and budget-friendly. For weekend evenings, we recommend calling ahead at (209) 954-9729 to ensure a table is ready when you arrive. You can also explore our full Japanese dining experience or order online for a cozy sushi night at home.
