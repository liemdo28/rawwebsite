<p>A sushi dinner is a solid choice for a birthday or milestone celebration — there's enough variety on the menu that a mixed group can order family-style, and a few house details make it easy to build a celebration around.</p>
<h2>Bringing a Cake</h2>
<p>If you want to bring a cake for the table, you're welcome to — there's a $12 fee to have it served, which is worth knowing ahead of time so it's not a surprise on the bill.</p>
<h2>Ordering for the Table</h2>
<p>For a celebration, it often works well to order a mix of appetizers to share (edamame, gyoza, or one of the tempura options), a few rolls from the standard list, and one or two of the <a href="/inside-raw-sushi-bars-signature-special-rolls-stockton.html">signature special rolls</a> for the group to split. That way, no one is stuck with just their own plate.</p>
<h2>Reserving Your Table</h2>
<p>Since we don't run online reservations, call (209) 954-9729 ahead of time, especially if your group is larger than four — check splitting is available for parties of four or fewer, so it helps to know in advance whether the group is splitting the bill or one person is covering it.</p>
<h2>Keeping It Simple</h2>
<p>A celebration dinner doesn't need to be complicated to feel special. Knowing the cake policy and reserving ahead covers most of what makes a group celebration go smoothly.</p>
