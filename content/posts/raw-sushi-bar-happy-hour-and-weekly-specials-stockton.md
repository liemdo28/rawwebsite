<p>Raw Sushi Bar keeps a consistent weekly happy hour and specials schedule. Here's exactly what's currently posted on our menu, so you can plan a visit around it instead of guessing.</p>
<h2>Happy Hour Hours</h2>
<p>Happy hour runs Monday through Thursday from 4:30 to 6:00 PM, and Friday through Sunday from 2:30 to 5:30 PM.</p>
<h2>What's on the Happy Hour Menu</h2>
<p>During happy hour, $4 gets you a sake bomb, small hot sake, Sapporo draft, or domestic beer. For $5, there's edamame, wakame salad, or a glass of house wine. At $7, you can order fried gyoza, fried shumai, a California roll, agedashi tofu, or a hand roll (spicy tuna or California). And for $9, there's a full spicy tuna roll, fried California roll, a Tsunami margarita, or a mule.</p>
<h2>Weekly Day Specials</h2>
<p>Beyond the daily happy hour window, Tuesday brings $6 hand rolls, and Wednesday features 50% off selected wine bottles while supplies last.</p>
<h2>A Few Things to Keep in Mind</h2>
<p>Prices are subject to change without notice, and sales tax applies to all transactions. If you're coming as a group, check splitting is available for parties of four or fewer. These specials are a good fit whether you're stopping in after work or planning a casual weekend evening.</p>
