<p>Sushi restaurants aren't always an easy fit for vegetarian diners, but Raw Sushi Bar's menu has several options that don't involve meat or fish. Here's what's actually listed.</p>
<h2>Vegetarian Entrees and Appetizers</h2>
<p>Vegetarian Teriyaki (seasonal vegetables in teriyaki sauce) is the clearest vegetarian entree on the menu. Vegetable Tempura and Agedashi Tofu (fried tofu in agedashi broth) are both vegetarian appetizers, and Edamame and Garlic Edamame are simple, reliable starters.</p>
<h2>Vegetarian Nigiri and Rolls</h2>
<p>Inari (a seasoned tofu pouch) is the vegetarian nigiri option. For rolls, a California Roll made without the crab, or asking about avocado and cucumber roll combinations, gives you a meat- and fish-free option — it's worth confirming specifics with your server, since roll composition can vary.</p>
<h2>Salads Without Seafood</h2>
<p>The Green Salad (mixed greens with house dressing) and Wakame Salad (marinated seaweed) are both meatless as listed. Sunomono, a cucumber and tomato salad in ponzu sauce, is another option worth asking about if you want to confirm it's served without added seafood.</p>
<h2>A Note on Asking</h2>
<p>Menus and preparations can change, so if you have a specific dietary need, it's always worth confirming details with your server rather than assuming — that's true at any restaurant, not just ours.</p>
