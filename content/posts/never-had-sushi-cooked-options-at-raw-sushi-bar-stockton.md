<p>Not everyone walks into a sushi restaurant ready for raw fish, and that's a normal way to start. Raw Sushi Bar's menu has a full section of cooked entrees for exactly that reason.</p>
<h2>Cooked Entrees on the Menu</h2>
<p>The entrees section includes Beef Teriyaki, Chicken Katsu (panko-breaded and fried, served with tonkatsu sauce), Chicken Teriyaki, Sesame Chicken, Grilled Salmon, Salmon Teriyaki, Shrimp Teriyaki, and Vegetarian Teriyaki. All of these come served with rice and a green salad.</p>
<h2>Cooked Appetizers Too</h2>
<p>If you want to ease in with a smaller plate first, Mixed Tempura, Vegetable Tempura, Fried Gyoza, and Fried Shumai are all cooked appetizers that give you a feel for the menu without any raw fish involved.</p>
<h2>You Can Still Sit at a Sushi Restaurant</h2>
<p>Ordering a fully cooked meal here doesn't mean missing out — it just means starting where you're comfortable. A lot of first-time guests order a cooked entree the first visit and try a roll or two the next time, once they've gotten a feel for the place.</p>
<h2>When You're Ready to Try Raw</h2>
<p>Whenever you decide to try something raw, a simple roll like a California roll (avocado and imitation crab, no raw fish) is a gentle first step before nigiri or sashimi.</p>
