<p>Beyond the standard rolls, Raw Sushi Bar's menu has a "Raw Special Rolls" section — a set of house-created combinations you won't find listed the same way anywhere else. Here's what's actually in a few of them.</p>
<h2>The Finest Roll (Reggie's Roll)</h2>
<p>This is the most built-out roll on the menu: avocado, panko shrimp, and spicy snow crab inside, topped with salmon, hamachi, garlic mayo, shiso, tataki ponzu, sriracha, red onion, tobiko, green onion, and tempura jalapeno. It's a lot of components layered onto one roll, built for someone who wants to try several flavors at once.</p>
<h2>Dante's Inferno Roll</h2>
<p>Tempura shrimp and avocado inside, topped with sesame seared tuna, fresh salmon, tempura jalapenos, green onion, and tobiko — a good pick if you liked the idea of heat and seared tuna together.</p>
<h2>Beef Cilantro Roll</h2>
<p>A departure from the usual seafood-forward rolls: cucumber, steamed asparagus, avocado, and cilantro inside, topped with sautéed beef, vegetables, and crispy rice. It's worth trying if you're dining with someone who wants something besides fish.</p>
<h2>Roadkill Roll and SCS Roll</h2>
<p>The Roadkill Roll pairs spicy tuna, avocado, and asparagus with a topping of BBQ eel, sesame tuna, crab salad, tempura bits, and tobiko. The SCS Roll layers tempura asparagus and spicy tuna with tempura scallops, more spicy tuna, avocado, spring mix, and tobiko on top — both are built for guests who want maximum toppings in one order.</p>
<h2>How to Choose</h2>
<p>If you're new to the special rolls, it helps to think about what you already like: seafood-forward and spicy (Roadkill, SCS, Dante's Inferno), rich and elaborate (The Finest Roll), or something with beef instead of fish (Beef Cilantro). Prices and full ingredient lists for all the special rolls are on the current menu.</p>
