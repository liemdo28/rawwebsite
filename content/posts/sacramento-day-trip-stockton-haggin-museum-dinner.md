<p>Stockton is about 49 miles from Sacramento, roughly an hour via I-5, with Elk Grove sitting along that route. For visitors planning a day trip, Stockton's downtown waterfront area has enough to fill an afternoon before dinner.</p>
<h2>What to See Downtown</h2>
<p>The Haggin Museum, set in Victory Park, holds fine art and local history collections and is a reasonable anchor for an afternoon visit. Downtown Stockton's waterfront district, near Banner Island Ballpark and Adventist Health Arena, is walkable and worth exploring on foot.</p>
<h2>Ending the Day With Dinner</h2>
<p>After a few hours of sightseeing, Raw Sushi Bar at 10742 Trinity Parkway Suite D, Stockton, CA 95219 is a straightforward dinner stop before the drive back to Sacramento or Elk Grove. Calling (209) 954-9729 ahead is worth it if you want a table waiting rather than an unplanned wait after a full day out.</p>
<h2>Making It a Full Day Rather Than a Rushed One</h2>
<p>Since the drive back is close to an hour, it's worth pacing the day so dinner doesn't turn into a rushed stop right before getting back in the car. Treating dinner as the actual close of the day, not an afterthought, makes the trip feel complete rather than hurried.</p>
