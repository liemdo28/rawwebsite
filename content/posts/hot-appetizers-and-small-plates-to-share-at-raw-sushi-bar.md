<p>Edamame gets a lot of attention, but Raw Sushi Bar's hot appetizer section has more range than most people expect from a sushi restaurant. Here's what's worth knowing before your next visit.</p>
<h2>The Fried Favorites</h2>
<p>Mixed Tempura (shrimp and vegetables) and Vegetable Tempura are the most familiar starting points. Squid Vinegar — fried squid served with ponzu sauce — is a good pick if you want something a little different from standard tempura.</p>
<h2>For the Table to Share</h2>
<p>Beef Asparagus (beef-wrapped asparagus in ponzu sauce with jalapeno and tomato) and Beef Carpaccio (thinly sliced, topped with fried veggies in ponzu sauce) both bring beef into what's otherwise a seafood-heavy menu — useful if your group has mixed preferences.</p>
<h2>Seafood Starters Beyond Sushi</h2>
<p>The Spider Appetizer (fried soft shell crab) and Fried Oyster (breaded, served with tartar sauce) are both cooked seafood options, which makes them a good bridge for guests who aren't ready for raw fish yet. Grilled Salmon Appetizer — two lightly salted, grilled pieces — is another simply prepared option.</p>
<h2>Building a Shared Starter Course</h2>
<p>For a table of three or four, ordering two or three of these to share alongside edamame or a salad is an easy way to start the meal before moving into rolls or entrees.</p>
