Sushi Catering Services for Every Occasion

Raw Sushi Bar provides professional sushi catering throughout Stockton and the surrounding Central Valley. With over 20 years of experience preparing the freshest sushi in Stockton, our team knows how to create a catering spread that impresses guests and makes any event memorable. From elegant wedding receptions to casual office lunches, we tailor every catering order to fit your occasion, guest count, and budget.

We also offer teriyaki plates, tempura trays, edamame, salads, and other Japanese dishes that round out a complete spread. How to Book Catering with Raw Sushi Bar

Booking sushi catering in Stockton is easy. Start by calling us at (209) 954-9729 or emailing info@rawsushibar.com with the details of your event: the date, estimated guest count, venue location, and any dietary preferences or restrictions. Our catering coordinator will work with you to design a custom menu and provide a detailed quote.

We recommend booking at least one week in advance for smaller events and two to three weeks for larger gatherings such as weddings or corporate conferences. This allows our chefs time to source the freshest ingredients and plan the preparation schedule. For last-minute requests, give us a call and we will do our best to accommodate you.
