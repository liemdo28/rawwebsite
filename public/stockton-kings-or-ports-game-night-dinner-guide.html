Catching a Stockton Kings or Ports Game? Here's Your Dinner Plan

Heading to a Stockton Kings or Stockton Ports game? Raw Sushi Bar is a dinner option worth planning around game night.

Stockton is home to two local teams worth planning a night around: the Stockton Kings (NBA G League) at Adventist Health Arena, and the Stockton Ports (Minor League Baseball) at Banner Island Ballpark. Both venues sit in Stockton's downtown waterfront area.

Building Dinner Into Game Night

If you're coming in from Elk Grove, Sacramento, or elsewhere in the region for a game, it's worth deciding ahead of time whether you want a sit-down dinner beforehand or a faster order to fit your schedule. Raw Sushi Bar works either way — a full sit-down meal if you have time before tip-off, or an online order for pickup if you're tight on time.

Ordering for a Group Before the Game

For a group headed to the same game, ordering a mix of rolls and appetizers to share is a straightforward way to feed everyone without a long, drawn-out meal. Our hot appetizers guide covers good starter options if you're short on time.

Planning Around Traffic and Parking

Downtown Stockton gets busier on game nights, so building in extra time — or ordering ahead for pickup rather than dining in — can help you avoid rushing your meal before heading to the arena or ballpark.
