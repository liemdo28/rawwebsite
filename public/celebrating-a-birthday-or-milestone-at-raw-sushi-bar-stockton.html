Celebrating a Birthday or Milestone at Raw Sushi Bar in Stockton

Planning a birthday or milestone dinner in Stockton? Here's what to know about bringing a cake, reserving a table, and ordering for the group.

A sushi dinner is a solid choice for a birthday or milestone celebration — there's enough variety on the menu that a mixed group can order family-style, and a few house details make it easy to build a celebration around.

Bringing a Cake

If you want to bring a cake for the table, you're welcome to — there's a $12 fee to have it served, which is worth knowing ahead of time so it's not a surprise on the bill.

Ordering for the Table

For a celebration, it often works well to order a mix of appetizers to share (edamame, gyoza, or one of the tempura options), a few rolls from the standard list, and one or two of the signature special rolls for the group to split. That way, no one is stuck with just their own plate.

Reserving Your Table

Since we don't run online reservations, call (209) 954-9729 ahead of time, especially if your group is larger than four — check splitting is available for parties of four or fewer, so it helps to know in advance whether the group is splitting the bill or one person is covering it.

Keeping It Simple

A celebration dinner doesn't need to be complicated to feel special. Knowing the cake policy and reserving ahead covers most of what makes a group celebration go smoothly.
