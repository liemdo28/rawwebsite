A Sacramento Day-Trip Guide: Haggin Museum, Downtown Stockton, and Dinner

Planning a day trip from Sacramento? Stockton's Haggin Museum and downtown waterfront pair well with dinner at Raw Sushi Bar, about 49 miles away.

Stockton is about 49 miles from Sacramento, roughly an hour via I-5, with Elk Grove sitting along that route. For visitors planning a day trip, Stockton's downtown waterfront area has enough to fill an afternoon before dinner.

What to See Downtown

The Haggin Museum, set in Victory Park, holds fine art and local history collections and is a reasonable anchor for an afternoon visit. Downtown Stockton's waterfront district, near Banner Island Ballpark and Adventist Health Arena, is walkable and worth exploring on foot.

Ending the Day With Dinner

After a few hours of sightseeing, Raw Sushi Bar at 10742 Trinity Parkway Suite D, Stockton, CA 95219 is a straightforward dinner stop before the drive back to Sacramento or Elk Grove. Calling (209) 954-9729 ahead is worth it if you want a table waiting rather than an unplanned wait after a full day out.

Making It a Full Day Rather Than a Rushed One

Since the drive back is close to an hour, it's worth pacing the day so dinner doesn't turn into a rushed stop right before getting back in the car. Treating dinner as the actual close of the day, not an afterthought, makes the trip feel complete rather than hurried.
