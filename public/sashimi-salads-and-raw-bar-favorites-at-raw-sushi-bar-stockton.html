Sashimi Salads and Raw Bar Favorites at Raw Sushi Bar Stockton

Beyond a plain sashimi plate: a look at the sashimi salads and raw bar dishes on Raw Sushi Bar's Stockton menu.

If you already like sashimi, the menu has a handful of dishes that build on it beyond a straightforward plate of sliced fish. Here's what's available.

Sashimi Salads

The Sashimi Sunomono pairs assorted sashimi with a cucumber salad and garlic chips. The Sashimi Wakame Salad does the same with marinated seaweed instead, and the Sashimi Salad serves assorted sashimi over a vinaigrette salad with green onion and tobiko. All three are heavier than a side salad but lighter than a full entree.

Ceviche and Poke

Tuna or Salmon Ceviche comes spicy, served with fried wonton chips. The Ahi Poke Martini serves tuna poke in a martini glass — a good option if you want something composed and a little different from a standard plate.

A Few More Raw Bar Options

The Spicy Lomi Lomi (fresh salmon and red onion with chili oil, ponzu, and garlic chips) and Spicy Sesame Tuna (seared, with jalapeno, garlic chips, chili oil, and ponzu) both bring some heat into the raw bar section. Tako Kimchee — octopus, cucumber, and tomato in kimchee sauce — is a less common pick worth trying if you've had the more familiar dishes already.

Building a Raw Bar Course

These dishes work well as a course of their own, ordered before rolls or entrees, especially for a table that already knows they like sashimi and wants to explore how it's prepared beyond a plain plate.
