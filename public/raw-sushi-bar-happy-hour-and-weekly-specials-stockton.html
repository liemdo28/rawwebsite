Raw Sushi Bar's Weekly Happy Hour and Specials Schedule in Stockton

Here's the real weekly happy hour and specials schedule at Raw Sushi Bar Stockton, straight from the menu — times, days, and what's included.

Raw Sushi Bar keeps a consistent weekly happy hour and specials schedule. Here's exactly what's currently posted on our menu, so you can plan a visit around it instead of guessing.

Happy Hour Hours

Happy hour runs Monday through Thursday from 4:30 to 6:00 PM, and Friday through Sunday from 2:30 to 5:30 PM.

What's on the Happy Hour Menu

During happy hour, $4 gets you a sake bomb, small hot sake, Sapporo draft, or domestic beer. For $5, there's edamame, wakame salad, or a glass of house wine. At $7, you can order fried gyoza, fried shumai, a California roll, agedashi tofu, or a hand roll (spicy tuna or California). And for $9, there's a full spicy tuna roll, fried California roll, a Tsunami margarita, or a mule.

Weekly Day Specials

Beyond the daily happy hour window, Tuesday brings $6 hand rolls, and Wednesday features 50% off selected wine bottles while supplies last.

A Few Things to Keep in Mind

Prices are subject to change without notice, and sales tax applies to all transactions. If you're coming as a group, check splitting is available for parties of four or fewer. These specials are a good fit whether you're stopping in after work or planning a casual weekend evening.
