Your Neighborhood Sushi Restaurant on I Street

Downtown Modesto is a vibrant neighborhood filled with local shops, galleries, and restaurants, and Raw Sushi Bar is proud to be part of this community. Our location at 1200 I Street puts us right in the center of downtown, making us an easy walk from offices, residences, and nearby attractions throughout the area.

Whether you work downtown and need a fresh sushi lunch, or you are heading out for a Saturday evening dinner, Raw Sushi Bar is the perfect spot. Our I Street location offers a comfortable, modern dining room where you can enjoy hand-crafted sushi rolls, delicate sashimi, and a full menu of Japanese dishes without leaving the neighborhood.

We have been serving the downtown Modesto community with dedication, building a loyal following of regulars who know that the freshest sushi in Modesto is right here on I Street. With over 791 Yelp reviews and a 4.5-star rating, our guests consistently rank us among the best restaurants in the downtown district.

Lunch & Dinner in Downtown Modesto

Weekday lunch. Join us Tuesday through Friday from 11:30 AM to 2:00 PM for a satisfying midday meal. Our lunch menu features the same fresh sushi, sashimi, and Japanese entrees that we serve at dinner, making it easy to enjoy a quality meal during your lunch break.

Dinner service. Our dinner hours run Monday through Saturday, with extended hours on Friday and Saturday evenings until 10:00 PM. Dinner is the perfect time to explore our full menu, including our most popular signature rolls and chef specials.

Takeout convenience. Short on time? Call us at (209) 566-9560 to place a takeout order and pick it up at our I Street location. We prepare every takeout order with the same care and attention as our dine-in plates, so you can enjoy Raw Sushi Bar quality wherever you are.

A Downtown Modesto Dining Destination

Raw Sushi Bar is more than just a restaurant — we are a gathering place for the downtown Modesto community. Couples enjoy date nights over shared sushi platters. Coworkers meet for Friday evening dinners after a long week. Families celebrate milestones with our generous combination plates. Whatever the occasion, our welcoming atmosphere and exceptional food make every visit special.

Our convenient I Street location means you are never far from great sushi when you are in downtown Modesto. Stop by for dine-in, or call ahead for quick takeout. We look forward to welcoming you to Raw Sushi Bar.
