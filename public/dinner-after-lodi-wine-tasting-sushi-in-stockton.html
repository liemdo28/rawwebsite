Dinner After a Day of Lodi Wine Tasting: Sushi in Stockton

Spent the day wine tasting in Lodi? Raw Sushi Bar in Stockton is about 17 miles and 19 minutes away for dinner afterward.

Lodi's wine country draws a steady stream of day-trip visitors, and by early evening, most tasting rooms are closing up. If you're looking for dinner afterward, Stockton is about 17 miles and roughly 19 minutes south down CA-99 — close enough to fold into the same day without much extra driving.

Why Sushi Pairs Well After a Wine Day

After an afternoon of tasting, a lot of visitors want something lighter than a heavy sit-down dinner. Sashimi, nigiri, and rolls are easy to order in whatever quantity fits your evening, and dishes like our sake and beer pairings (detailed on our sake and sushi pairing guide) work well if you're not quite ready to stop drinking for the day.

Making the Stop Easy

Since you'll likely be driving in from a day of tasting, it's worth calling ahead to (209) 954-9729 to put your name on the list, especially on a weekend evening when tables fill up faster. We're at 10742 Trinity Parkway Suite D, Stockton, CA 95219, just off the main Trinity Parkway corridor.

A Practical Central Valley Combination

Lodi and Stockton sit close enough together that pairing a wine country afternoon with a Stockton dinner is a genuinely convenient plan, not a long detour. It's a combination a lot of regional visitors already make without necessarily planning it in advance.
