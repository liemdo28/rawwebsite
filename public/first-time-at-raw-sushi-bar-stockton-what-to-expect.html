First Time at Raw Sushi Bar? Here's What to Expect in Stockton

Never been to Raw Sushi Bar in Stockton before? Here's what a first visit actually looks like, from parking to ordering.

Walking into a sushi restaurant for the first time can feel like a lot to figure out at once. Here's a simple rundown of what an actual visit to Raw Sushi Bar at 10742 Trinity Parkway Suite D, Stockton, CA 95219 looks like.

Getting There and Getting Seated

We're located in the Trinity Parkway retail corridor, with parking right in front. For groups or weekend evenings, it's worth calling (209) 954-9729 ahead to reserve a table, since we don't run online booking.

Reading the Menu

The menu is organized into clear sections: small bites, hot appetizers, sushi appetizers, salads, entrees, nigiri, sushi rolls, and our special rolls. If raw fish isn't your thing yet, the entrees section has cooked options like teriyaki and katsu dishes, so there's no pressure to order something unfamiliar on your first try.

A Reasonable Way to Start

A lot of first-time guests start with a simple roll like a California roll or spicy tuna roll, add an appetizer like edamame or gyoza to share, and go from there once they know what they like. There's no wrong order — the point of a first visit is figuring out your own preferences, not following a strict script.

Good to Know Before You Sit Down

Check splitting is available for parties of four or fewer. Outside food and drinks aren't permitted, though corkage is available for wine at $18 a bottle. And since we serve raw fish, we're required to note that consuming raw or undercooked seafood carries a foodborne illness risk — if that's a concern, our kitchen has cooked dishes too.
