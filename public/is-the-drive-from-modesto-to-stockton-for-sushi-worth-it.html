Is the Drive from Modesto to Stockton Worth It for Sushi? An Honest Look

Modesto to Stockton is about 29-33 miles, roughly 35-40 minutes. Here's an honest look at whether that drive is worth it for dinner.

Modesto to Stockton is roughly 29 to 33 miles, about 35 to 40 minutes depending on traffic and route. That's a real drive, not a quick errand — so it's fair to ask whether it's worth it just for dinner.

When It Makes Sense

If you're already planning to be in Stockton for something else — visiting family, running an errand, catching a Stockton Kings or Ports game — folding in dinner at Raw Sushi Bar adds very little extra time. It also makes more sense for a special occasion than a random Tuesday, simply because of the round trip.

When It Might Not

If you're looking for a quick weeknight dinner and don't have another reason to be in Stockton, a 35-to-40-minute drive each way is a genuine ask. That's a fair trade-off to weigh honestly rather than pretend away.

Making It Worth the Trip

If you do make the drive, it helps to call (209) 954-9729 ahead to confirm a table, and to plan for a fuller meal — rolls, a few appetizers, maybe one of the signature special rolls — rather than a quick bite, since you've already committed the drive time.

The Honest Answer

It's worth it if you're combining the trip with another reason to be in Stockton, or treating it as an occasion rather than a routine weeknight stop. For a quick meal on a random evening, the drive is a real factor to consider.
