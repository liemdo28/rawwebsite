About Raw Sushi Bar in Stockton

Raw Sushi Bar is one of the best sushi restaurants in Stockton, California. Founded in 2005 by Hoang, who grew up around traditional Japanese cuisine, Raw Sushi Bar was created to bring high-quality, authentic Japanese food to the Stockton community. What started as a small sushi bar has grown into one of the most loved Japanese restaurants in Stockton and the Central Valley.

Our sushi chefs hand-select the freshest fish daily and prepare every piece with precision and care. From classic nigiri and sashimi to creative signature rolls like the Dante's Inferno and BTS Roll, every dish at Raw Sushi Bar reflects 20 years of dedication to quality. We also serve savory ramen, Japanese entrees like teriyaki and tempura, and craft cocktails.

Whether you're searching for the best sushi in Stockton for a date night, a family dinner, or a quick takeout order, Raw Sushi Bar delivers an exceptional dining experience every time. We offer dine-in service, takeout, and online ordering for pickup. Delivery is available through DoorDash.
