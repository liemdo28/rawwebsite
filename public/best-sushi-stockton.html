Why Raw Sushi Bar Serves the Best Sushi in Stockton

When locals search for the best sushi in Stockton, one name comes up again and again: Raw Sushi Bar. With over two decades of experience and a relentless commitment to quality, our restaurant has earned its reputation as the top sushi destination in Stockton and the greater Central Valley. Every piece of fish is hand-selected for freshness, and our chefs bring years of traditional Japanese training to every plate.

What sets Raw Sushi Bar apart from other sushi restaurants in Stockton is our dedication to sourcing. We work directly with trusted suppliers to ensure that our salmon, tuna, yellowtail, and specialty fish arrive at peak quality. Nothing sits around. From the delivery truck to your plate, freshness is our standard, not a marketing promise. That commitment is why guests keep coming back and why we maintain a 4.5-star rating across more than 655 Yelp reviews.

Beyond the fish, our kitchen team prepares perfectly seasoned sushi rice, house-made sauces, and crisp tempura that elevate every roll. Whether you prefer a traditional Rainbow Roll, a bold Dante's Inferno Roll, or a simple plate of salmon sashimi, you will taste the difference that 20 years of craft makes. Visit us at our Stockton location and find out for yourself.

Customer Favorites & Must-Try Rolls

Our guests have spoken, and these are the dishes that keep Stockton coming back. The BTS Roll, loaded with spicy tuna, avocado, and our signature crunch, is one of the most popular items on the menu. For something with heat, the Dante's Inferno Roll delivers bold flavor with every bite. Sashimi lovers swear by our Chef's Choice Sashimi Platter, featuring the freshest cuts of the day.

If you are new to sushi, our staff is happy to guide you through the menu and recommend rolls that match your taste. We also offer teriyaki bowls, tempura plates, and udon noodles for guests who want authentic Japanese cuisine beyond sushi. And do not miss our Happy Hour specials, available Monday through Thursday from 4:30 to 6:00 PM and Friday through Sunday from 2:30 to 5:30 PM, for discounted rolls and drinks.

Ready to taste the best sushi Stockton has to offer? Order online for pickup, call us at (209) 954-9729, or stop by 10742 Trinity Parkway, Suite D. We look forward to serving you.
