Ordering Sushi for the Office: A Team Lunch Guide for Stockton

Planning a team lunch order for a Stockton office? Here's how to order sushi for a group ahead of time for pickup.

Ordering sushi for a team lunch is a straightforward way to feed an office without everyone driving separately. Here's how to make it work smoothly.

Ordering Ahead

The most reliable approach is placing your order online in advance and scheduling a pickup time that fits your team's schedule, rather than calling in a large order last minute. That gives the kitchen time to prepare everything properly instead of rushing a big order.

Building a Mixed Order

Offices usually have mixed preferences, so it helps to order a range: a few standard rolls (California, spicy tuna), one or two signature special rolls for the more adventurous eaters, and a cooked option like teriyaki or tempura for anyone who'd rather skip raw fish. Our cooked-options guide is worth sharing with the team beforehand.

Handling the Bill

If one person is expensing the order, a single online order keeps it as one transaction. If the team is splitting costs individually, it's worth deciding that ahead of time — check splitting in-house is available for parties of four or fewer, but a large takeout order works differently since it's picked up rather than served at a table.

Timing the Pickup

Building in a buffer before your scheduled pickup time helps avoid a rushed walk back to the office with food that's already been sitting.
