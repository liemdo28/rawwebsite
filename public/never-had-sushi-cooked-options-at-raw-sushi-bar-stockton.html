New to Sushi? Cooked and Non-Raw Options at Raw Sushi Bar Stockton

Never tried sushi, or just not in the mood for raw fish? Raw Sushi Bar's Stockton menu has several fully cooked entrees and appetizers.

Not everyone walks into a sushi restaurant ready for raw fish, and that's a normal way to start. Raw Sushi Bar's menu has a full section of cooked entrees for exactly that reason.

Cooked Entrees on the Menu

The entrees section includes Beef Teriyaki, Chicken Katsu (panko-breaded and fried, served with tonkatsu sauce), Chicken Teriyaki, Sesame Chicken, Grilled Salmon, Salmon Teriyaki, Shrimp Teriyaki, and Vegetarian Teriyaki. All of these come served with rice and a green salad.

Cooked Appetizers Too

If you want to ease in with a smaller plate first, Mixed Tempura, Vegetable Tempura, Fried Gyoza, and Fried Shumai are all cooked appetizers that give you a feel for the menu without any raw fish involved.

You Can Still Sit at a Sushi Restaurant

Ordering a fully cooked meal here doesn't mean missing out — it just means starting where you're comfortable. A lot of first-time guests order a cooked entree the first visit and try a roll or two the next time, once they've gotten a feel for the place.

When You're Ready to Try Raw

Whenever you decide to try something raw, a simple roll like a California roll (avocado and imitation crab, no raw fish) is a gentle first step before nigiri or sashimi.
