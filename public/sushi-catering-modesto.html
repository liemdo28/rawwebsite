Raw Sushi Bar offers professional sushi catering services throughout Modesto and the surrounding Central Valley. Our catering menu features the same fresh, high-quality sushi that has earned us a 4.5-star rating and over 791 reviews on Yelp. Every platter is prepared by our experienced sushi chefs using the freshest fish and finest ingredients, ensuring that your catered event delivers the same exceptional quality our dine-in guests enjoy.
