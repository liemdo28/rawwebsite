A Manteca Family's Guide to Dinner at Raw Sushi Bar in Stockton

Manteca families looking for a sushi dinner out: Raw Sushi Bar in Stockton is about a 15-20 minute drive via CA-99.

Manteca sits about 15 to 17 miles from Stockton, a drive of roughly 20 minutes up CA-99 and CA-4. For families deciding where to go for dinner, that's close enough to make Raw Sushi Bar a realistic option without an evening built entirely around driving.

Something for Everyone at the Table

Not every kid — or every adult — wants raw fish, and that's fine. The menu includes cooked entrees like Chicken Katsu, Chicken Teriyaki, and Vegetable Tempura alongside the sushi and sashimi, so a family with mixed preferences can order together without anyone settling for something they don't want. Our cooked-menu guide breaks these down further.

Making Room for a Group

If your family is larger than a table for two, it's worth calling (209) 954-9729 ahead, especially for weekend evenings. Check splitting is available for parties of four or fewer, which helps if you're coordinating who's paying for what.

Worth the Short Drive

Twenty minutes is a manageable ask for a family dinner out, especially if it means everyone at the table — from picky eaters to adventurous ones — finds something they actually want to order.
