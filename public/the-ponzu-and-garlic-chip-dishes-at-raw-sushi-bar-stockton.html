The Ponzu and Garlic Chip Dishes That Define Raw Sushi Bar's Style

Ponzu sauce and garlic chips show up across Raw Sushi Bar's Stockton menu. Here's where, and why it's worth trying if you like that flavor combination.

If you've ordered one dish in ponzu sauce with garlic chips at Raw Sushi Bar and liked it, there's a whole thread of the menu built around that same combination worth exploring.

Where It Shows Up

Albacore Vinegar (thinly sliced seared white tuna in ponzu with garlic chips), Yellowtail Razor (thinly sliced yellowtail sashimi with jalapenos in ponzu), and Assorted Sashimi Ponzu all use the same base combination with different fish.

Beyond the Appetizer Section

The Sashimi Sunomono and Sashimi Wakame Salad both pair sashimi with garlic chips as well, moving the combination into salad territory. Spicy Lomi Lomi and Spicy Sesame Tuna both add ponzu and garlic chips alongside chili oil for more heat.

Why the Combination Works

Ponzu's citrus-forward acidity cuts through raw fish's richness, and garlic chips add a crunchy, savory contrast. It's a consistent enough pairing across the menu that it functions almost like a house style, rather than a one-off garnish.

A Way to Explore the Menu

If you liked one of these dishes, ordering another from this same list is a reliable way to try something new without stepping too far outside what you already know you enjoy.
