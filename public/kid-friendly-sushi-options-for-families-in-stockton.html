Kid-Friendly Sushi Options for Families in Stockton

Bringing kids to a sushi restaurant in Stockton? Here are the menu items at Raw Sushi Bar that tend to work well for younger eaters.

Bringing children to a sushi restaurant can feel uncertain if you're not sure what they'll actually eat. Here's what tends to work well for younger eaters at Raw Sushi Bar, based on what's actually on the menu.

Cooked Options Kids Tend to Like

Chicken Katsu (breaded and fried, served with tonkatsu sauce) and Chicken Teriyaki are both familiar, approachable dishes for kids who aren't ready for raw fish. Both come with rice and a green salad.

A Gentle Introduction to Rolls

A California Roll — avocado and imitation crab, no raw fish — is often an easy starting point for kids curious about sushi rolls without jumping straight to raw fish. Edamame is also a simple, familiar side a lot of kids already recognize.

Planning the Meal

If your family has mixed preferences, ordering a cooked entree or two alongside a few rolls for the adults means everyone has something they actually want to eat, without the whole table compromising.

A Reasonable Family Dinner Option

Sushi restaurants aren't only for adults with adventurous palates — with the right order, it's a workable dinner option for families with younger kids too.
