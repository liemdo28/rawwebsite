Highway 99 Road Trip? Stockton Is a Practical Dinner Stop

Driving Highway 99 between Sacramento and the southern Central Valley? Stockton sits roughly in the middle, making it a practical dinner stop.

Highway 99 runs through the heart of the Central Valley, connecting Sacramento to Stockton, Modesto, and points further south toward Fresno. Stockton sits along that route, roughly 49 miles south of Sacramento, making it a natural place to break up a longer drive.

Why Stop in Stockton

If you're driving the 99 corridor and want a real meal rather than fast food off the highway, Stockton has more sit-down options than some of the smaller towns along the route. Raw Sushi Bar is just off the main Trinity Parkway corridor, easy enough to reach without a long detour.

A Quick Stop or a Longer Break

If you're only stopping briefly, ordering ahead online for pickup means you're not waiting once you arrive. If you have more time to spend, a sit-down meal is a good way to actually rest partway through a longer drive rather than eating in the car.

What Travels Well

If you're getting food to eat later down the road, rolls and nigiri hold up reasonably well over a short additional drive, since they're meant to be served cold to begin with.
